You are calibrating a dataset that gives English words physical properties, for an interactive piece where typed words fall and land like objects.

Rate each word below on five independent axes. Every score is a number from -1.0 to 1.0, to one decimal place.

## The axes

**mass** — how heavy it feels. -1 feather, +1 boulder.
Ladder: boulder +1.0 · anvil +0.8 · stone +0.5 · book +0.2 · apple 0.0 · leaf -0.3 · feather -0.6 · mist -0.9 · breath -1.0

**restitution** — how much it bounces on landing. -1 clay, +1 rubber.
Ladder: rubber +1.0 · ball +0.8 · spring +0.5 · wood +0.2 · bone 0.0 · sand -0.3 · clay -0.6 · mud -0.9 · ash -1.0

**warmth** — thermal and tonal temperature. -1 ice, +1 ember.
Ladder: ember +1.0 · hearth +0.8 · amber +0.5 · bread +0.2 · stone 0.0 · silver -0.3 · frost -0.6 · ice -0.9 · glacier -1.0

**age** — how old the thing the word denotes feels. -1 fresh, +1 ancient.
Ladder: primordial +1.0 · ruin +0.8 · parchment +0.5 · oak +0.2 · house 0.0 · plastic -0.3 · neon -0.6 · pixel -0.9 · debut -1.0

**intensity** — how loud and forceful it feels. -1 quiet, +1 scream.
Ladder: scream +1.0 · blast +0.8 · shout +0.5 · speak +0.2 · walk 0.0 · murmur -0.3 · whisper -0.6 · hush -0.9 · silence -1.0

## Rules

1. Score the felt quality, not the literal fact. Abstract words have no physical mass, but they have an imagined one. "grief" is heavy. "whim" is light. Every word gets five numbers regardless of whether it denotes an object.

2. Use the whole range. If every word lands between -0.2 and +0.2 the dataset is worthless. Most words sit in the middle, but populate the tails.

3. THE AXES MUST NOT TRACK EACH OTHER. Before finalizing a word, check whether you have copied "mass" into the other four axes with the sign flipped. If heavy words are always loud and old in your output, then the other axes are doing no work.

4. intensity is ACOUSTIC ENERGY, not importance. A boulder is enormous and completely silent — near 0. A cathedral is imposing and is the quietest building most people ever enter — near 0 or below. An alarm weighs nothing and is deafening — near +1. Grandeur, significance and scale are not intensity.

5. warmth is temperature, not pleasantness. "fever" is warm and unpleasant. "silver" is cool and desirable. Do not score how nice the word is.

6. age is about the thing, not the word. "ruin" is ancient even though the word itself is ordinary English.

7. Function words and grammatical particles ("the", "of", "which") have no referent. Score them near 0.0 across the board rather than inventing character for them.

8. Never refuse or skip. Every word in the list gets a row, including offensive ones — this is a lexical dataset, not an endorsement.

## Output

Return a single JSON array with one object per word, in the order given. No commentary.

[
  {"word": "boulder", "mass": 0.9, "restitution": -0.5, "warmth": 0.0, "age": 0.4, "intensity": 0.0}
]
